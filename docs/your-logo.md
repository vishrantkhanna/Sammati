# Your Logo

Show your organisation's logo on your consent forms and manage pages, so
people know who is asking.

## Add It

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Under **Your Logo**, choose **Upload a Logo** and pick the file.

It appears straight away in the admin top bar, at the top of every form,
on the page shown after someone agrees, and on the manage page.

## What Works Best

- PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 256 KB.
- A wide logo about 160 by 48 pixels, on a transparent or white
  background.
- SVG files are not accepted, because an SVG can carry script.

To change it, upload a new file. To take it off, choose **Remove Logo**.
Without a logo, your organisation name is shown instead.

## Where Sammati Appears

Once you add a logo, your logo leads:

- **Admin top bar:** your logo, with a small "Powered by Sammati" beside it.
- **Forms and manage pages:** your logo at the top, and a small "Powered
  by Sammati" line with the Sammati mark at the bottom.
- **Sign-in screen and admin footer:** the Sammati logo.

The "Powered by" line links to this project. It sends nothing to anyone:
it is an ordinary link, and Sammati has no tracking.
