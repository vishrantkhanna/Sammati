# Create Your First Form

A form needs two things first: a purpose and a notice.

## 1. Add a Purpose

A purpose is one specific reason you use personal data.

1. Open **Purposes**.
2. Enter a code such as `newsletter`. It cannot be changed later.
3. Enter a title and a simple description, for example "Send you our
   monthly newsletter by email."
4. Choose **Add Purpose**.

Keep purposes separate. "Newsletter" and "Offers from partners" are two
purposes, so people can agree to one and not the other.

## 2. Publish a Notice

The notice is what people read before they agree.

1. Open **Notices**.
2. Enter a name such as `newsletter`.
3. Write the English title and text. Say what you collect, why, how long
   you keep it, how to withdraw, and how to complain.
4. Add Hindi or another language if your audience needs it.
5. Choose **Publish**.

A published version can never be edited. To change the wording, publish
again under the same name. Sammati keeps every version and stores, with
each consent, the version that person saw.

## 3. Create the Form

1. Open **Forms**.
2. Enter a name (used in the link) and a title.
3. Pick the notice.
4. Tick the purposes to offer. Mark one **Required** only when your service
   cannot work without it.
5. Choose **Create Form**.

The form now has a link, such as `http://localhost:8787/f/newsletter`.
Open it and try it.

## 4. See the Record

Open **Records**. Search for the email you entered to see that person's
consents and their private manage link. **Verify Records** checks that no
record has been altered.

## How Withdrawal Works

After agreeing, a person is shown a private link. It opens a page that
lists their consents with a **Withdraw** button beside each. Send the same
link in your confirmation email. You can also record a withdrawal for them
from **Records** when they ask you directly.
